Privacy and data

This page explains what Patchipelago stores, what third-party services may process, and how to exercise your rights.

The game works without an account. Progress, preferences and the tutorial status are saved on your device. Ads and payments depend on the installed version.

Publisher and contact

Publisher: GolApp Studio. For privacy questions or data access and deletion requests: golappstudio.support@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Local storage saves virtual credits, progress, workshops, research, releases, rewards, language, audio preferences and tutorial completion. The current version does not send these game records to a Patchipelago server. Uninstalling may remove them.

Preview / testing version

Patchipelago Preview uses fake ads and free simulated purchases. These simulators do not trigger real Google Play transactions or AdMob ads. Simulated entitlements stay on the Preview app and cannot be transferred to Production.

Production / advertising

Production may use optional Google AdMob rewarded ads after configuration. Google Mobile Ads and its UMP consent platform may process device identifiers, technical ad delivery data and consent preferences depending on your choices and applicable law. Watching ads is not required to play. Real ads are not enabled in the current prototype.

In-app purchases

Optional Production packs will use Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; Patchipelago does not access complete card information. A secure server will need to check product identifiers and purchase tokens through Google Play before granting an entitlement. This service is not deployed yet, so real payments remain disabled.

Retention and deletion

Game saves remain on the device while app data is retained. You may clear Android app data or uninstall. Google may separately retain real ad and purchase data under its policies. Contact us concerning any future Patchipelago purchase verification backend.

Your choices and rights

Change language or mute sound/music at any time. Rewarded ads are optional; where legally required, advertising privacy choices will be offered. Contact us for access, rectification, objection or deletion requests regarding data under GolApp Studio's control. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Third parties and links

Potential Production providers include Google AdMob, Google UMP and Google Play. Their policies are linked below. GitHub Pages hosting may create technical web logs under GitHub's own policies.

Updates

Last updated: October 28, 2026. This notice describes the prototype. We will review it before launching real monetization, including verification server provider details and retention periods.
